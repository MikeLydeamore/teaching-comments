## How access works

Hosted spaces are not created automatically for every account. An Ed.ie administrator can create one for you, or an existing owner can invite you to a space as an editor or owner.

Access belongs to your signed-in Ed.ie account. Participants do not need hosted-space access to join a session.

## Accept an invitation

1. Sign in with the account the owner invited.
2. Open the account menu and select **Invitations**.
3. Find the hosted space under **Pending invites**.
4. Check the offered role, then select **Accept**.

The space appears under **Your spaces** after acceptance. Selecting **Decline** removes the pending invitation without granting access.

## If you do not see any hosted spaces

The most likely explanation is that no space has been shared with the account you used to sign in.

Check that:

- you signed in with the intended Google or GitHub account;
- **Invitations** does not contain a pending invitation;
- the owner used your exact Ed.ie username or the email address associated with your sign-in; and
- you have refreshed **Your spaces** after accepting.

If there is no invitation, ask the space owner to check the pending member entry or send a new invitation. If your class needs a new hosted space, ask the Ed.ie administrator for your deployment.

## Understand your role

An **Editor** can enter the hosted space and run sessions. An **Owner** can also invite, remove, and change members. Your role appears on the space card under **Your spaces**.
