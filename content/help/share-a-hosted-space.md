## Invite a co-host

Only an owner can change membership.

1. Open the hosted space and select **Manage access**.
2. Under **Invite a co-host**, enter the colleague’s exact Ed.ie username or email address.
3. Choose **Editor** or **Owner**.
4. Select **Send invite**.

The colleague sees a pending invitation under **Invitations** and gains access after accepting it.

## Choose the right role

An **Editor** can enter the hosted space and run its sessions. An **Owner** can do that and can also invite, remove, or change members.

Grant owner access only to people who should manage the teaching team. Keep at least one active owner so the space cannot be left without someone able to manage access.

## Change or remove access

Open **Manage access** and use the controls beside the member. Owners can change a member’s role, remove active access, or deal with a pending invitation.

Removing someone prevents future access; it does not rewrite activity they previously helped run.

## Work in the same session

Multiple hosts can open the same live session. Prompt changes, session access, timers, moderation, and display settings are shared.

For a smooth class, agree who controls the prompt and projector. A second host can concentrate on screening responses and group questions.

## Troubleshoot an invitation

- For a username, use the exact Ed.ie username.
- For an email, use the address associated with the colleague’s Ed.ie sign-in.
- If they already belong to the space, manage their existing member row instead.
- If you see **View-only access**, ask a current owner to make the change.
